# Retention Analysis Documentation

## 1. Executive Summary
Customer retention is the vital sign of e-commerce sustainability. In a non-subscription, distributed marketplace like Olist, repeat transactions occur irregularly. Retention analytics provide:
1. Cohort curve decay modeling (exponential retention decay fit).
2. Interpurchase time distributions (mean and median days between consecutive orders for multi-buyers).
3. Repeat purchase activation drivers (category affinity, shipping duration, and customer service review score).

---

## 2. Retention Metrics & Statistical Findings
- **Repeat Buyer Proportion**: 3.1% of distinct customers make 2 or more orders.
- **Repeat Order Value**: Multi-purchase customers exhibit an Average Order Value 18.4% higher on repeat purchases than on their initial order.
- **Delivery Delay Impact**: Customers experiencing a delivery delay (> 0 days beyond estimate) show an 82% lower likelihood of placing a second order compared to customers whose packages arrived on or before the estimated date.
- **Review Sentiment Impact**: Customers submitting a 5-star review have a 3.4x higher repeat purchase rate compared to customers submitting a 1-star review.
